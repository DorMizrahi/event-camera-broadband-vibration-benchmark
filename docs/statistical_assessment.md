# Statistical assessment

The primary endpoint is x-acceleration and the secondary endpoint is x-velocity. One paired observation per trajectory compares methods on exactly the same reconstructed displacement. The raw two-sided Wilcoxon p-value is 3.7252903×10⁻⁹ for each endpoint. Both have W=1 and 29 positive differences when a positive sign denotes a gradient improvement. Rank-biserial correlation is 463/465. It measures signed rank dominance. Median paired reductions are 10.84% and 4.92%, respectively. The report gives the absolute NRMSE context.

The historical Holm family spans overall and within-family quantity/axis comparisons, including vector acceleration. Of 36 eligible rows, 35 yield a finite p-value in the recorded environment. The undefined all-zero overall x-displacement comparison is excluded. Reconstructing a two-test family would change the p-values. Baseline percentile bootstrap intervals resample 30 experiments, 100,000 times with seed 2027. Diagnostic trimming intervals use 10,000 draws. These are conditional summaries of case-to-case variability.

Wilcoxon inference conventionally requires independent paired differences across units and a symmetric distribution of differences for a location-shift interpretation. Different seeds do not establish random sampling of cases, exchangeability over the factor allocation, or symmetry. The two derivative paths on one trajectory are intentionally paired. The 30 prescribed settings, common generator, sparse design and family-specific components restrict generalization. The four Chirps should be presented individually with descriptive summaries.

Only 21 of 270 nominal four-factor cells are occupied. The categorical main-effects matrix has rank 12 of 16. Contrast uniquely identifies displacement in this allocation. Family, axes and spectral content have partial overlap, insufficient for independent design-based causal conclusions. An unregularized full model is nonidentifiable; a hierarchical fit would require extra assumptions and poorly informed group estimates. Report stratified medians, case counts and the complete allocation. Emphasize “within these 30 prescribed synthetic cases” when interpreting a paired result.
